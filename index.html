Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use our store, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you browse our site or place an order, we may collect details such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and order history. This information is used only to process and deliver your orders and to provide customer support.

How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us confirm orders, arrange delivery, respond to your messages, and improve our products and services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Order and account details are stored securely and are only accessible to store administrators for the purpose of fulfilling your orders.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out using the contact details on our Contact page.

Return Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us. Please review our return conditions below.

Eligibility

Due to the nature of fragrance products, we can only accept returns for items that are unopened, unused, and in their original packaging, reported within a reasonable time of delivery.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us with photos of the product as soon as possible so we can arrange a replacement or refund.

How to Request a Return

Reach out to us through the contact details on our Contact page with your order number and reason for return.

Shipping Policy

We aim to get your fragrances to you as quickly and safely as possible.

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver nationwide. Delivery charges and estimated delivery times may vary depending on your location, and are shown at checkout.
